# M3 Public Beta Terms, Privacy, and Safety Notice

Last updated: 2026-07-11

This document is a practical beta-stage notice for M3. It combines basic terms
of use, privacy information, consent language, and safety limits. It is a draft
for review and is not legal advice.

Before capture, M3 asks you to confirm that you are 18 or older and explicitly
accept the current notice version. If you do not agree, do not send information
to the bot.

## 1. Public Beta Scope

M3 is an experimental, publicly discoverable, approval-gated beta for approved
adult users only. It is not a public launch, clinical service, therapy service,
medical device, crisis service, or emergency support tool.

Access may be approved, paused, limited, or removed by the M3 operator during
the beta. The service may change, break, lose features, or stop at any time.

## 2. What M3 Does

M3 helps users record structured CBT-oriented episode notes through Telegram and
generate reflective summaries, reports, maps, and analytics from those records.

M3 is intended for personal reflection and product/model experimentation during
the beta. It does not provide diagnosis, medical advice, psychiatric advice,
therapy, crisis assessment, or professional treatment.

## 3. Sensitive Information Warning

M3 may process information that users consider personal, emotional, behavioral,
or health-related. You should avoid sharing information that you are not
comfortable storing or processing through an experimental beta service.

Do not submit information about other people unless you have the right to share
it and you understand that it may become part of your stored beta records.

## 4. Emergency And Crisis Use

Do not rely on M3 if you believe you may harm yourself or someone else, or if
you need urgent medical, psychiatric, or emergency assistance.

In those situations, contact local emergency services or appropriate
professional support immediately.

M3 is not designed to detect, prevent, or respond to emergencies.

## 5. Adult-Only Use

M3 is intended only for adults who have been approved for the beta. Do not use
M3 if you are under 18 or if you are not authorized to use the beta.

You are responsible for what you choose to send to the bot and for deciding
whether the service is appropriate for your own use.

## 6. Data M3 May Collect

When you use M3, the service may collect and store:

- Telegram identifiers and profile metadata needed for access control.
- Messages, replies, and answers you send to the bot.
- Structured episode records generated from your answers.
- Private capture artifacts, confirmed transcripts, and extraction/debug
  sidecars used to prepare episode drafts.
- Derived classifications, summaries, reports, maps, and analytics.
- UX event metadata, such as step names and timestamps, without raw answer text.
- Userlist, approval, pause, and admin records.
- Consent version, decision, adult confirmation, and decision timestamp.
- Technical logs or debugging information needed to operate the beta.

## 7. How Data Is Used

M3 may use collected data to:

- run the Telegram bot and manage access;
- save and structure episode records;
- generate summaries, reports, maps, and analytics;
- debug, administer, and secure the beta;
- evaluate product usability, quality, safety, and usefulness during the beta;
- improve the structure, prompts, reports, and model workflow.

The M3 operator does not sell your data and does not use it for advertising or
marketing.

## 8. Experimental Analytics Disclaimer

Reports, summaries, insights, patterns, maps, or visualizations may be
incomplete, inaccurate, misleading, outdated, or generated in error.

Treat M3 outputs as reflective prompts, not objective assessments of you, your
health, your mental state, your relationships, or your future behavior.

Do not make medical, legal, financial, safety, or other high-stakes decisions
based only on M3 outputs.

## 9. Telegram And Third-Party Processing

M3 operates through Telegram. Telegram is the message transport, and your use of
Telegram is also governed by Telegram's own terms and privacy policies.

The M3 owner or admin may manually review stored records, userlist records,
reports, and technical logs to operate the beta, resolve issues, improve
quality, or respond to user requests.

For `/1t`, `/3b`, and `/1a`, confirmed text may be sent to the configured
DeepSeek API to structure an episode draft. Profile wording may send prepared,
content-reduced analytics artifacts to DeepSeek. Raw audio is processed locally
when the configured Whisper path is used and is not retained by M3. Owner tools
may also process selected records for quality review, debugging, or analysis;
third-party tools are subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

## 10. Data Sharing Limits

The M3 operator will not intentionally share your personal beta records for
advertising, marketing, or sale.

Data may be shared or accessed when reasonably needed for:

- Telegram message transport;
- AI or software tool processing described above;
- owner/admin operation of the beta;
- security, debugging, backup, or maintenance;
- responding to your export or deletion request;
- complying with legal obligations or protecting safety.

## 11. Data Retention, Export, And Deletion

The operator must publish the selected retention period before approving the
public-beta release. Until that decision is recorded, M3 makes no automatic
expiry promise and the public-beta gate remains blocked.

You may request export or deletion of your active records through the Telegram
contact or admin contact that approved your beta access.

The operator tooling can inventory, export, delete, and verify configured local
runtime records by Telegram identity. Deletion also clears affected derived
snapshots and shared reports/exports/backups that cannot be safely attributed.
Copies outside configured runtime paths, including external backups or prior
handoffs, require separate operator follow-up and cannot be verified by the
runtime tool.

## 12. Security

M3 is intended to use private storage and limited access during the beta, but
no system is perfectly secure. Do not send information that would cause serious
harm if accessed, disclosed, corrupted, or lost.

You are responsible for securing your Telegram account, devices, and local
access to your messages.

## 13. Your Content

You keep ownership of the information you send to M3.

By using M3, you give the M3 operator permission to store, process, structure,
analyze, summarize, review, and use your submitted content as needed to operate,
evaluate, and improve the approval-gated beta.

This permission is limited to the beta purposes described in this document. It
does not give the M3 operator permission to sell your personal records or use
them for advertising.

## 14. Changes To This Notice

This notice may be updated as M3 changes. A changed configured notice version
requires explicit acceptance again before capture continues.

For material changes, the M3 operator should notify active beta users through
Telegram or the normal beta contact path.

## 15. No Warranty

M3 is provided as an experimental beta service. It is provided "as is" and "as
available" without promises that it will be accurate, available, secure,
complete, useful, or error-free.

## 16. Contact

For questions, access issues, export requests, deletion requests, or privacy
concerns, contact the M3 operator through the Telegram chat or admin contact
that invited or approved you for the beta. The current product contact shown by
the bot is `@mesto3`.
