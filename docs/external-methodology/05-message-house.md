# Message House

## Main Idea

MISHA helps people notice repeated patterns in concrete life episodes.

## Problem

People often cannot see a pattern while they are inside it. Each episode feels
separate, even when similar thoughts, emotions, bodily reactions, behaviors, or
consequences keep returning.

## Solution

Structured episode capture helps users decompose concrete moments and turn
them into a cautious map of possible recurring links.

## Trust

MISHA reflects user-provided material with cautious language. It keeps the
user's agency intact and does not replace professional help.

## Technology

Under the hood, MISHA builds structured episode records and compares them over
time. Reports show possible recurring links and the episodes they are based on.

## Beta Position

MISHA is an early product. Its reflections are provisional and should be used
for self-observation, not formal assessment.

## Emotion

Not "something is wrong with you," but "now you can observe what repeats."
