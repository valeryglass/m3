# Message House

## Main idea

MISHA helps people notice recurring inner scenarios.

## Problem

People often cannot see a pattern until it has repeated many times. Each
episode feels separate while the same reaction may be returning in a new
situation.

## Solution

A structured chat captures concrete episodes and turns them into a map of
recurring links.

## Trust

MISHA does not diagnose, pressure, or replace a professional. It reflects
user-provided material with cautious language and keeps the user's agency
intact.

## Technology

Under the hood, MISHA builds structured episode records and a map of recurring
links.

## Emotion

Not "something is wrong with you," but "now you can see what repeats."
