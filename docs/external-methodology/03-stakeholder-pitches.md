# Stakeholder Pitches

## For investors

MISHA is a personal pattern map for self-understanding.

Many consumer mental wellness products capture mood, offer generic advice, or
simulate conversation. MISHA focuses on a different layer: structured personal
data from concrete life episodes. It helps users build a clearer view of
recurring emotional, cognitive, and behavioral links over time.

The product value is trust and continuity. Users do not only receive replies;
they gradually build a reflection asset grounded in their own described
episodes. That creates room for habit formation, explainable insights, and a
possible future bridge to professional conversations.

MISHA should not be presented as clinically validated unless that validation
exists. It should not promise medical outcomes, treatment, diagnosis, or
replacement of professional care.

## For doctors and psychologists

MISHA is a structured episode journal.

It helps people record concrete examples from daily life: what happened, what
they thought, what they felt, how they reacted, what they did, and what
followed. Over time, it can help users notice recurring links and bring clearer
material into professional conversations.

This can support better self-reporting and between-session reflection. Instead
of arriving with a vague summary, a user may have specific episodes and
recurring observations to discuss.

MISHA does not diagnose. MISHA does not treat. MISHA does not replace doctors,
psychologists, therapists, or clinical judgment. Its role is to help structure
user-provided material and reflect repeated links cautiously.

## For technical stakeholders

MISHA is a structured data product, not a chatbot wrapper.

The core product idea is episode structure: concrete situations are captured in
a form that can be compared over time. MISHA keeps a clear boundary between
what the user described and what the system later reflects back as a possible
recurring link.

The output should be explainable to the user. A pattern is not presented as a
hidden truth about personality. It is framed as a recurring link based on
described episodes.

At a high level, MISHA combines privacy-sensitive design, structured
self-observation, and pattern mapping. Its credibility depends on careful
language, visible grounding in user material, and avoiding claims that belong
to clinical tools.

## For users

MISHA is a gentle mirror for repeated loops.

It helps you write down concrete moments from life and see what may be
repeating across them. You may notice that similar tension appears before
postponing, that mistakes often connect with self-criticism, or that certain
conflicts lead to the same kind of shutdown.

The point is not shame. The point is recognition. MISHA does not diagnose you,
judge you, or tell you who you are. It helps you see what your own episodes may
be showing again and again.

Sometimes relief begins when a vague feeling becomes a visible pattern.
