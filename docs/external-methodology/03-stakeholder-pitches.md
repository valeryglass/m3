# Stakeholder Pitches

## For Investors

MISHA is a beta-stage personal pattern map for self-observation.

Many consumer mental wellness products capture mood, offer generic advice, or
simulate conversation. MISHA focuses on a different layer: structured personal
data from concrete life episodes. It helps users build a clearer view of
repeated emotional, cognitive, bodily, and behavioral links over time.

The product value is trust, continuity, and repeated use. Users do not only
receive replies; they gradually build structured material grounded in their own
described episodes. That creates room for explainable reports, habit formation,
and a possible bridge to better-prepared professional conversations.

MISHA should be presented as an early self-observation product unless stronger
validation exists. It should not promise health outcomes or replace
professional care.

## For Doctors And Psychologists

MISHA is a structured episode journal for user self-observation.

It helps people record concrete examples from daily life: what happened, what
they thought, what they felt emotionally, what they noticed in the body, how
they behaved, what happened soon after, and what continued later. Over time, it
can help users notice possible recurring links and bring clearer material into
professional conversations.

This may support more concrete self-reporting and between-session reflection.
Instead of arriving with a vague summary, a user may have specific episodes and
recurring observations to discuss.

MISHA does not provide clinical judgment or urgent support. Its role is to help
structure user-provided material and reflect possible repeated links
cautiously.

## For Technical Stakeholders

MISHA is a structured data product, not a chatbot wrapper.

The core product idea is episode structure: concrete situations are captured in
a form that can be compared over time. MISHA keeps a clear boundary between
what the user described and what the system later reflects back as a possible
recurring link.

The output should be explainable to the user. A pattern is not presented as a
hidden truth about personality or a cause. It is framed as a possible recurring
link based on described episodes.

At a high level, MISHA combines privacy-sensitive design, structured
self-observation, repeated-use pattern mapping, and cautious reporting. Its
credibility depends on careful language and visible grounding in user material.

## For Users

MISHA is a gentle structure for noticing repeated loops.

It helps you write down concrete moments from life and see what may be
repeating across them. You may notice that similar tension appears before
postponing, that mistakes often connect with self-criticism, that certain
conflicts lead to withdrawal, or that a bodily signal appears before a familiar
reaction.

The point is not shame. The point is recognition. MISHA does not judge you or
tell you who you are. It helps you look at what your own episodes may be
showing again and again.

Sometimes clarity starts when a vague feeling becomes a specific episode you
can observe.
